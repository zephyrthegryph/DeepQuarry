### F5: gas watches

A holder that must not poll its air declares one of two things. Both are Rust-driven: nothing in DM reads the gas to find out that it changed.

| Need | Form | Delivered as | Where the edge is found |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Tell me every change of this part of the gas" (a gauge, a sleeping device waking, a material service that wants the numbers) | `gas_watch(air =, mask =, changed =)` for the holder's own air; `gas_watch_many()` / `gas_watch_ids()` for a device that sleeps on several mixtures | The observation record of the change (`x(list/observation, index)`, or `x(watch, mixture_id, change_mask, observation, index)` for the many form) | Rust: a native gas watch per handle, fed by the mixture's revision counter |
| "Is the pressure / temperature / moles past this level" (a relief valve, a thermal fuse, a sensor) | `gas_level(into =, reading =, above = or below =, hysteresis =, air =)` and a tracked var on the holder | The tracked var `into` (TRUE while past the level); the holder reacts with `on_change(nameof(var), ENTER / EXIT / ANY, parts...)` | Rust: a threshold watch on the mixture's cell (`om_world_when()`); DM hears only crossings |

**Why a level is a tracked var and not a notice.** A level is a state ("the air is too hot"), not an occurrence. Notices are ordered and never coalesced (section 10); a state that changes in bursts is a tracked value (section 7), and the reaction to it is an `on_change`, which already runs its parts once per drain however many times the var moved and takes `at_most = t` to space the runs. That is the coalescing a burst of crossings needs, so there is no new coalescing form: Rust merges the wakes of one watch in one tick, the tracked setter ignores a write of the value it holds, and `on_change` runs once per drain. A holder with several levels gives each its own `into`; a holder wanting several bands declares several levels.

**Why Rust finds the edge.** The Rust gas domain already compares a channel with a level on every change it sees, with hysteresis and both-edge reporting (`Cond::Threshold`, `verdigris/core/src/watch/mod.rs`), for turf cells, machine-owned mixtures and pipe regions alike. DM then does work only at a crossing: a reading that moves 0.5 kPa a tick under its level costs DM nothing, where a stream watch wakes the holder on every revision band move and compares in DM. A stream watch is still the right form when the holder wants the numbers (a material service accounting corrosion) or has a rule no single level states (a disposal bin's "can I draw air now"). Moving the comparison into the native gas watch record would duplicate the threshold facility, so no Rust change was needed.

**The guarantee that holds under both.** Both forms ride the Rust mirror of the watched mixture. The mirror of a watched pipe region is refreshed from the network's own region revision (`refresh_pipe_mirrors`, `verdigris/ffi/src/gas/mix.rs`), so a write that reaches Rust without DM noticing (a heat exchanger or a pipe device writing the region, a heat write that changes only the temperature) is seen by the watch. `dq_gas_level_hears_pipe_region_heat` and `dq_gas_watch_hears_heat_writes` pin it. Nothing in DM notes a write for a watch.

**Following the mixture.** The level is armed on the mixture in the holder's `air` var (or its turf's air), and `atmos_air_set()` re-arms it when the var is pointed at another mixture. A holder on a turf calls `gas_level_rearm_all(src)` from `Moved()`. A holder with no mixture reads FALSE; a level that starts past its limit turns its var at arming.

**Retired.** `om_watch_arm_bands`, `_revision`, `_raw`, `_derived`, `om_watch_recheck`, `om_watch_lookup`, `om_watch_disarm_keys`, `/datum/om_watch_band`, `watches_gas()` (the legacy capability: no production holder used it) and `om_watch_gas()`. The artifact (air past `ARTIFACT_HEAT_BREAK`) is the first `gas_level()` holder; the disposal bin and the material service sleep on `gas_watch_many()` / `gas_watch_ids()`. Cost note: the material service now holds one native gas watch per mixture it watches instead of sharing one per mixture through the old hub; the DM-side wake count is the same, Rust evaluates one more watch per service.

**Machine callers retired.** The firedoor watches hot/cold temperature levels on its center and neighboring mixtures and pressure changes with `gas_watch_many()`; the airlock sensor uses a pressure `gas_watch()`. Their lifecycle follows the native watch APIs. Machine-service and world legacy gas-watch bookkeeping is gone, and `code/datums/om/watch.dm` and `/datum/om_gas_watch_hub` are deleted. The unrelated legacy `om_watch()` change-channel API is outside this gas-watch retirement.

Replacements that **exist** and only need caller conversion: `wait()`/`silent_wait()`/`claims()` for `om_task_timed`/`om_busy` (prove `claims()` covers `om_busy`); `open_request`/`asks` for simple `om_ask`; `ACTION()`/`PUBLISH` for `OM_EMIT`; `every()` for `DECLARE_PERIODIC`/`DECLARE_REPEAT` (after C2/C3); `/datum/io/*` for `om_io`; `granted_verb()` and `grant()` for `om_grant`.

### Proc-gate parking (rewrite/om-fields)

`every(when = PROC_REF(x))` parks on the tracked reads of the gate proc (the generated reads table `stat_generated_reads()`, the same table a requirement or a stat contribution reads) and wakes when any of them publishes. A gate proc that reads untracked state is a `sem/reads unknown_read` / `unannotated_global` error at build time. A gate with a relation hop (`host.stat`) still polls, because the wake does not subscribe through a hop; a gate that throws keeps polling (nothing would wake it). `dq_gap/every_with_a_proc_gate_parks` pins it; contagion's spread lane is the first user (its gate reads only the strain's own tracked state; whether the host is dead now is the step's check).

### K-U: why tgui windows still use the OM wakes

(Owned by the UI-outputs lane; nothing converted here.)

`/datum/tgui` binds a watch on `CHANGE_GENERIC_MASK` of its host (`om_ui_bind`) and its user's mob channels (`om_ui_status_bind`). ### H4 design: branching ban flows as asks() steps (not implemented)

Written for review (rewrite/om-leftovers). Today `topic_unbane`, `topic_unbanf`, the ban panel, the player-panel buttons, the shuttle time edit and the legacy player notes
re-run the href with an answer slot ("a4", "a5", "a6", ...) through `ban_topic_ask()` / `topic_rerun_ask()` (115 sites). The branching is plain DM between the asks.

What already exists: an op's `asks()` steps run in order, each can carry `when = PROC_REF(x)` (x(datum/act/op/A) reads the step is reached; a false `when` skips the step with no prompt
and leaves `A.answer` as it was), and the answers are read with `A.step_value("step")`. That covers the two-level branch of the unban-edit flow:

- step "temp" `asks(/datum/prompt/choice/admin_ban_topic, fields = list("question" = "Temporary Ban?", "choices" = list("Yes", "No")), step = "temp")`
- step "minutes" `asks(/datum/prompt/number/admin_ban_topic, ..., step = "minutes", when = PROC_REF(ban_is_temporary))`, where `ban_is_temporary` is `A.step_value("temp") == "Yes"`
- step "reason" `asks(/datum/prompt/text/..., step = "reason")`, and the handler (`then()`) does the write once, with every answer read from the steps.

What is missing, and is the one framework addition H4 needs: a step that repeats. The per-job ban asks one question per banned job until the admin stops, and the
answers are a list. Proposed part: `asks(..., step = "job", repeats = PROC_REF(more_jobs))`: after each answer the engine calls `more_jobs(datum/act/op/A)`; TRUE asks the
step again (the field computation re-runs, so the question can name the next job), FALSE moves on. The answers are kept in order, `A.step_values("job")` returns the list, and
`keeps = WAIT_KEEPS_DEFAULT` governs the abandoned flow as for any step. A cancel at any step ends the op with nothing written, as today.

Order of work once the repeat part exists: (1) the unban/edit pair, (2) the add-ban flow including the IP ban branch (`when =` on the IP step), (3) the per-job loop, (4) the player-panel
buttons and shuttle time edit (single-question flows; they need only `asks()`), (5) delete `topic_rerun_ask()`, `ban_topic_ask()` and the `rerun` of `/datum/prompt_rerun/topic`, and ban the names.
Tests: one per flow, driving `test_ui()` with `test_answer()` for each step including a cancel mid-flow (no ban written) and a "No" at the temporary step (permanent ban).
